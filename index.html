Questions

Before you put it out.

What happens after the 2 free pickups?

The can stays in your menu bar and keeps counting, but the truck stops coming. Your Trash goes back to normal and stays put until you empty it yourself. Buy a license and pickups start again.

Can I get a file back?

Yes, any time before pickup. Click the can, find the file in the pile and choose Show in Finder, then drag it out of the Trash. After pickup it's gone, the same as emptying the Trash yourself.

Can I skip a week?

Yes. Choose Skip this week on the last call notification, or click the pickup day at the top of the Trash Day panel. You can also pause service from Settings.

Is this a second trash can?

No. The can is a window into your Mac's real Trash. Delete things the way you always have.

Will it speed up my Mac?

No, and we won't pretend it does. It clears out the clutter you trashed and forgot about, like old screenshots, installers and screen recordings, and gives you that disk space back.

Why isn't it on the Mac App Store?

App Store apps can't ask Finder to empty the Trash. Selling it directly lets Trash Day use one small Finder permission instead of Full Disk Access.

What about Photos, Mail and iCloud?

Not yet. Trash Day empties the Finder Trash only.
