Refund policy

If it's not for you, you get your money back.

Last updated October 30, 2026

Try it first. Your first 2 pickups are free, no license key needed. That's two real weeks to see if Trash Day fits how you work.

14 days, no questions asked

If you buy a license and change your mind, email support@itstrashday.com within 14 days of purchase with your order number or the email you bought with. You'll get a full refund. You don't need to give a reason.

How refunds work

  • Orders are handled by Lemon Squeezy, our reseller and merchant of record. They process the refund back to your original payment method.
  • Most banks show the refund within 5 to 10 business days.
  • Once refunded, the license key is turned off and the app goes back to the free trial state. Your Trash stays put; nothing is deleted.

After 14 days

We'll still take a look if something went wrong, like a duplicate purchase or a key that never worked. Just write in.

Questions

Email support@itstrashday.com. A person reads every message.
